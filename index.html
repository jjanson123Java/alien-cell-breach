How to survive the grid

01

Choose a cell

Select any empty chamber. The grid locks while you fight for that location.

02

Counter the alien

Alien attackYour counter

Correct counters damage the alien. Incorrect choices damage you.

03

Claim the grid

Win a battle to place X. Lose to place O. Three X marks wins; three O marks loses. A full grid without a line is a draw.

04

Controls and reset

Use mouse, touch, or Tab plus Enter/Space. Closing Help preserves the game. Restart game erases the entire board and active battle.
